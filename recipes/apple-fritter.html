Apple Fritter

Photo by Jake Sternquist / Food Styling: Holly Dreesman / Prop Styling: Gabriel Greco

Description

These homemade apple fritters are crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, filled with chopped apples and a hint of cinnamon.

Fry spoonfuls of apple batter until golden brown, then toss in cinnamon sugar while still warm.

Ingredients

  • 1 quart vegetable oil for deep-frying
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2/3 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 3 cups apples - peeled, cored and chopped
  • 1 cup cinnamon-sugar

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients. Heat oil in a deep fryer or electric skillet to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  2. Stir flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl. Pour in milk, eggs, and oil; stir until well blended.
  3. Mix in apples until evenly distributed.
  4. Working in small batches to prevent overcrowding, drop spoonfuls of batter into the deep fryer and fry in hot oil until golden all over, about 5 minutes.
  5. Use a slotted spoon to transfer fritters to a paper towel-lined plate.
  6. Toss warm fritters with cinnamon sugar in a bowl until well coated.
  7. Enjoy!
